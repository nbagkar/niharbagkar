About Me

Hi, I'm Nihar — I build at the intersection of business, technology, and design. I love crafting ventures, solving meaningful problems, and scaling ideas from zero to impact.

📘 What I Study

I'm currently pursuing a B.S. in Business, Technology & Entrepreneurship at NYU Stern (GPA: 3.88), with a joint minor in CS & Math and a minor in Psychology. I'm interested in tech, venture capital, and finance, and I aspire to be in the technology and startup industry with the possibility of my own venture.
