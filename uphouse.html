§ 01 · The problem

Friend groups run on a dozen scattered chats.

Plans, birthdays, and decisions for a friend group tend to live in group chats, where they scroll away. UPHouse is built as one calm home for everything a crew shares, instead of a dozen scattered chats.

Every group gets its own private space. You create a group, share an invite code, and everyone lands in the same place.

§ 02 · What it does

One house for the whole crew.

Everything a group shares, in one app.

  • A home dashboard.

    Upcoming birthdays, your next event, and who is in the crew, at a glance.

  • A shared calendar.

    Create events and RSVP, so everyone knows the plan.

  • Forum and polls.

    Boards, threads, and reactions for conversations that do not get lost, and polls to settle a group decision quickly.

  • Trackers.

    Counters, streaks, leaderboards, and roster boards for a group’s inside stuff.

  • Daily games.

    A Games hub with a fresh puzzle each day to play together, such as Daily Word, Number Hunt, and Anagram Sprint.

  • More than one crew.

    A group switcher lets you belong to several groups and jump between them.

§ 03 · Design choices

Four choices that shaped it.

  • Invite-only, by design.

    There is no public feed and no strangers, only the people you add. Each group stays separate.

  • One home per crew.

    Each group has its own home, and a group switcher moves between them, so crews do not blur together.

  • Reminders that stay on your device.

    Birthday and event reminders are scheduled privately on the device, with no server or account needed. Birthdays can fire at 9 AM, noon, or 6 PM, and events from 30 minutes to a day ahead.

  • Something to do together.

    Daily puzzles with streaks, and trackers with leaderboards, give a group something to do together each day.

§ 04 · Where it stands

Version 1.0, just launched.

UPHouse launched on September 14, 2026 as version 1.0, a free app rated 4+. The App Store flags that it contains user-generated content.
