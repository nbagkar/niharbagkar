§ 01 · The problem

Finding a sound should not break your flow.

Cue was built around the friction of finding the right sound in the middle of making something. Behind it are 25+ producer interviews and an MVP that shipped in a single sprint cycle.

§ 02 · What it does

Say what you want to hear.

The app opens on one prompt: Search for your perfect sound.

  • Search in words.

    Describe a sound in language, and Cue translates the description into production-ready audio.

  • Quick starting points.

    Category chips such as Ambient, Music, Voice, and Effects, plus descriptors like Vintage, Warm, and Keys.

  • A home, a library, and new searches.

    A simple sidebar with Home, Library, and New Search.

  • Accounts and Premium.

    Search is behind a login, and the app has a Premium subscription.

§ 03 · Where it stands

Live, and early.

Cue is live, and searching requires an account. An MVP shipped in one sprint cycle, alongside 25+ producer interviews.
